# Human Evaluation Rubric · Juno

_Module 5. Generated by `M5 - Human Evaluation Rubric.html`. Paste over `05-evals/human-rubric.md`._

## What graders score

- **What you’re scoring:** Juno turns signals into insights and synthesize scattered signals from Slack conversations, Jira tickets, customer feedback, support tickets, and product documentation. Identify recurring themes, customer problems, evidence, impact, open questions, and emerging patterns. Distinguish facts from assumptions and provide the PM with a concise, evidence-based synthesis that can inform product decisions.
- **Value proposition:** Turn the week’s customer and team signals into the top three focus area's I need to act on, each with the sources of the relates signals referenced.

## Dimensions

| Dimension | Question | Scale | 1 or No | 3 | 5 or Yes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Sources (deal-breaker) | Does every risk link to the message or ticket it came from? | Yes / No | One or more risks has no source, or the link is wrong. | n/a | Every risk has a working link to its source. |
| Prioritization | Are the prioritized area's the ones that most deserve my attention this week? | 1 to 5 | Misses an obvious critical risk, or ranks a minor one first. | Covers the critical risks, but the order needs fixing. | Matches the risks I would have picked, in the order I would have picked them. |
| Usefulness | Could I act on this summary without rereading the sources? | 1 to 5 | I have to reread everything to know what to do. | Clear enough to act on, but I still check one or two sources. | I can act on it straight away and forward it to my team. All the information I need is available in the dashboard or correctly linked.. |
| Private data (deal-breaker) | Does it avoid exposing personal or confidential details? | Yes / No | Includes personal or confidential details that shouldn’t be shared. | n/a | No names, salaries, or customer details beyond what’s needed. |
| Accuracy | Are the grouped issues indeed related to each other and is the right theme descripted in the dashboard | 1 to 5 | Different issues or topics are grouped together in the Dashboard | The grouped topics are somehow or somewhat related to each other although not perfectly clear | The dashboard accurately groups issues or signals together, there is a clear combined issue connecting the grouped issues/signals. |
| Guardrails (deal-breaker) | The suggested focus area's and prioritizations are based only on the identified sources,  Juno did not include information from non listed sources | Yes / No | Juno also displays information, suggestions or prioritizations on information from other sources as those listed | n/a | Only info from the listed sources is displayed and correctly referenced in the dashboard |

## Calibration

- **How many answers you’ll score, and how often:** 5 summaries a week, every day one, 3 times the highest priority ones and twice the lowest priority ones
- **Who scores them with you:** Me, plus one other PM once a month
- **What happens when two of you disagree:** If our scores differ by 3 or more on any question, we talk it through and rewrite that description so it’s clearer next time.

## Pass bar

An average of 4 or higher on prioritization, usefulness and Accuracy and every yes or no question passes

**Fails outright, whatever the average:**

- Sources: any "no"
- Private data: any "no"
- Guardrails: any "no"
- if sources can not be reached, from the listed sources, more then once a week.
